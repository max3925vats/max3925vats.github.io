title: Monte Carlo Simulations for Polymer Chains
description: Monte Carlo simulation of freely-jointed polymer chains around a central spherical interface, in a periodic cubic box. The code measures how chains structure themselves near the sphere — radial density profiles, chain dimensions, and chain-shape metrics — as a function of chain length, sphere size, packing fraction, and (optionally) attractive interactions.
